What sets us apart · reservation control

Auto-expiring reservations: no property stuck on hold

Without a rule, a broker can hold a property indefinitely and block the sale for the whole team. Time limits prevent that, with no one having to chase anyone.

1 request

No duplicates

A broker can only have one request per property.

20 min

Office deadline

With no answer, the request expires and the property stays available.

2 hours

Time to close

Once approved, the broker has 2 hours to complete it, with a countdown.

24 hours

Block

If not completed, the property goes back to Available and that broker can't reserve it for 24 h.

The times are configurable. The values shown are the suggested default and can be set per development, according to your company's policy.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does the system work outside Brazil?

Yes. The platform runs in the cloud and is adapted during onboarding to your country's language, currency, documents and real estate rules. Today the interface is in Portuguese; the version in your language is part of the proposal.

Do I need to install a server?

No. The system runs in the cloud and your team just signs in. Brokers use a link on iPhone, Android and computers.

Can I keep using my CRM?

Yes. Where there is an integration, your CRM stays the official source and SKL turns that data into a visual, operational tool. Each integration is assessed case by case.

Can I see it working before deciding?

Yes. We run a live demo over video call, with the application working, and you receive a tailored proposal.
